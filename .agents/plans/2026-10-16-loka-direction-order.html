Decide what v1 is before branding it

Loka is already a native browser at alpha.10 (PR #14), but three threads planned against the old prototype on main. Fix that, then go in order, since each step feeds the next.

Needs your answer

  1. Review and merge PR #14 so main is the real browser? Every later step reads main.
  2. AI in v1: I recommend none built in, and an opt-in tab sorter later. Agree?
  3. Which “Aside” do you mean? I couldn’t tell from the threads.
  4. Start steps 1 and 2 now, in parallel?

Clean up, then six steps

  1. Make main true. Merge #14, close #3, #4, #6 and #8, and commit the five uncommitted pages. Gives: one source of truth for every agent.
  2. Learn how a browser works. Rewrite the architecture page against Loka’s real 7 patches. Gives: you can read each patch and say why it exists.
  3. Map the field. Arc, Dia, Comet, Zen, Atlas, Aside and others, feature by feature. Gives: every feature tagged “have”, “v1”, “later” or “never”.
  4. Define v1 and the AI stance. Who it’s for, the one promise, the feature cut, what “done” means. Gives: the filter for every choice after it.
  5. Brand. Pick a logo, sky, type and voice from what’s already drawn. Gives: one direction file that every mockup follows.
  6. UI/UX on the native browser. Close the audit gaps and build the v1 features. Gives: the v1 build.
  7. One landing page. Merge #5 and #15 into a single site that sells v1. Gives: a waitlist that sends signups somewhere real.

Steps 1 and 2 don’t depend on each other. Brand work (4) can start while UI (5) is underway.

Why this order: three things went wrong today

Two audits and the architecture review ran on main, which predates the planets, the horizon and the native browser. The architecture page says patches/loka/ is empty. On feat/native-sidebar it holds 7 patches, and spaces are built on Chromium tab groups.

Two landing pages exist (#5, live at loka-browser.vercel.app, and #15). Both were built before v1 or the logo was settled, so both will need redoing.

Brand work produced 9 logos, 7 skies, 9 styles and 6 concepts, with nothing to choose between them. A v1 promise is what makes that choice possible.

Leave AI out of v1

Loka promises calm tab sorting, as private as Helium. A built-in assistant sends page content to a model, and Dia, Comet and Atlas already race on that.

What AI could look like later, and what would change my mind

Funded teams are competing on agents. Loka’s opening is the calm, private Arc for Windows.

Later: an opt-in sorter that files open tabs into folders and names spaces, run on a local model or your own key, off by default. It serves the sorting promise instead of competing with Dia.

What would change this: if step 2 shows Arc users leaving mainly for Dia’s AI features, AI belongs in the v1 discussion. That evidence doesn’t exist yet.
