Things to try

  1. Switch spaces with the small planets either side of the name, or Ctrl+1 to 3. The orbit turns: the planet you're on shrinks into the neighbour spot it now belongs in, and the one you clicked grows out of its spot until it fills the horizon. The list is ready at once; the orbit takes 380ms, most of it in the first 200ms. Press quickly a few times: a burst of switches runs at 200ms each so it keeps up with you.
  2. Right-click anything: a pinned tab, an open tab, a folder, an Essential, the empty part of the list, the planet or its name, the page, or a row in the Archive. Shift+F10 opens the same menu from the keyboard.
  3. Open the ⋯ menu at the top left and pick Sidebar on the right. The sidebar and page swap sides.
  4. Right-click the planet and pick Edit space…. The color and planet change live as you pick. New space… flies you to the new world.
  5. Hover a pinned tab and press its minus. It greys out but stays put. Click it again: the page reloads and Back still works. The Lit tab under Docs has wandered off its pinned page (Lit docs); the curved arrow takes it home.
  6. Press Skip 6 hours ahead. Open tabs you haven't used in 12 hours go to the archive. The playing lofi stream in Home stays, and so do pinned tabs and Essentials.
  7. Compare the stars with Today's sky, which still drifts a pixel at a time.

Loka's setup is Helium's onboarding with two Loka pages added. Helium's pages keep their words, the prefs they write and the rules that skip them, so a Helium update merges cleanly.

Things to try

  1. Make two or three spaces. They join the orrery, and the big planet takes on what you're making.
  2. On Helium services, turn off the first switch or the extension proxy. The Password manager page drops out, and its dot goes with it.
  3. On Make it yours, pick Day: the setup window changes at once. Pick the right side and watch where the sidebar lands.
  4. Finish with Open Loka: the browser opens with your spaces, side, sky and tidy-up time, and your first planet flies in. Use defaults on the first page skips straight there with sample spaces.
