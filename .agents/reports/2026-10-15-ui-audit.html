The sidebar reads well; managing it doesn’t

I drove the prototype with the seed data, by mouse and keyboard. Scanning is calm; changing things is where people get stuck.

  1. Ctrl K finds your tabs first. Typing “lit” opens a search while “Lit docs” sits pinned.
  2. Spaces can be renamed and recolored. Today a new space stays “Space 4”.
  3. Close and Clear get Undo. Clear deletes every open tab for good.
  4. The keyboard works in menus and the tree. Escape and the arrow keys do nothing.

  1. Which mockups should I build, and in what order? Reply with numbers below, or “all, in order”.
  2. Track loaded and unloaded pinned tabs in the model now (mockup 5)? It adds a field to the saved state.

Method, and what I didn’t check

Assumed intent: people live in this all day sorting many tabs, so it should feel compact, instant and quiet. Findings are judged against that.

I drove the dev server with Playwright: every menu, rename, Ctrl K, all three spaces, 21 open tabs, a 900×560 window, and 30 Tab presses. Evidence is in .agents/verify-runs/20261016-032313-ui-audit/.

The Critly check and taste scripts can’t see inside Lit’s shadow DOM. I measured with the inventory script patched to walk shadow roots instead.

Not checked: drag and drop by hand (the verify-loka recipes cover it), screen readers, light mode (the app is dark only), and performance.
