How these were drawn, and what’s not done

Each mark is SVG on the same 64-unit grid as resources/loka/branding/product_logo.svg, using its sky gradient (#16344a to #091018), shade #224859 and lit #78cdf5. Planet gradients follow planet.ts.

Not done: Windows icon sizes (scripts/loka_brand.py assets draws those from the chosen mark), a check at 125% and 150% display scaling, and space colors drawn per hue instead of with hue-rotate.
